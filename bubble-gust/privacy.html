Bubble Gust Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Hookline & Sinker Games ("we") makes Bubble Gust. Bubble Gust does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts. Your high scores and settings (sound) are saved only on your device. If you delete the app, they are removed.

When you tap Share score, the score card is made on your device and goes only where you choose to send it from the share sheet. We never see it.

If this changes (for example, adding Game Center leaderboards, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy), we will update this page.
