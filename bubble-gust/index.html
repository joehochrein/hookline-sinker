Bubble Gust Support

Bubble Gust is an arcade game for iPhone and iPad by Hookline & Sinker Games. Bubbles blow in on the wind and sink toward the grass; pop them before they land. No spam. No ads. Just fun.

Need help?

Email hookline.sinker@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS version. You will get a reply as soon as possible.

Quick answers

How do I play? Slide a finger anywhere to drive the cannon left and right, and tap anywhere to shoot. To shoot while you drive, keep one finger sliding and tap with another. Let three bubbles touch the grass and the game is over.

Why did my cannon stop shooting? The barrel overheated. Holding fire heats it up, and when the meter under the cannon fills, it locks for a couple of seconds. Aimed shots stay cool, and the ❄️ Cool barrel power-up stops it heating at all.

What do the special bubbles do? Caged bubbles take two hits and score double. Green bubbles split into two smaller ones. A heart bubble gives you an extra life (one per wave). Gold bubbles hold a power-up: spread shot, slow motion, cool barrel or updraft.

How do I share my score? When a game ends, tap Share score to send a score card to friends from the share sheet.

How do I turn the sound off? Tap the speaker button in the top corner. Sound also follows your iPhone's silent switch.

Where are my scores saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
