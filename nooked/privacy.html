Nooked Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Hookline & Sinker Games ("we") makes Nooked. Nooked does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts. Your scores and settings (sound, haptics) are saved only on your device. If you delete the app, they are removed.

If this changes (for example, adding Game Center leaderboards, which Apple manages under its own privacy policy), we will update this page.
