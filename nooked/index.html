Nooked Support

Nooked is a block puzzle game for iPhone and iPad by Hookline & Sinker Games. Fit odd shapes in tight nooks. No spam. No ads. Just fun.

Need help?

Email nooked.support@icloud.com and include your device model and iOS version. You will get a reply as soon as possible.

Quick answers

How do I turn a piece? Tap the left or right side of a piece in the tray to turn it either way. In Options you can make each turn cost points for an extra challenge.

How is the Daily game different? Daily uses the same pieces for everyone on a given day, so you can compare scores with friends.

Where are my scores saved? Only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Where can I see the rules again? Open Options and tap How to play.
